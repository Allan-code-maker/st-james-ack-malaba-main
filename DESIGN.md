# Design Brief — St. James ACK Malaba

## Tone
Warm, welcoming, dignified. Feels like a real Kenyan church, not a tech startup.

## Colors
- Primary: Deep burgundy `#6B1A2A`
- Accent: Warm gold `#C9A84C`
- Background: Cream `#FAF6EF`
- Text: Dark charcoal `#1A1A1A`

## Typography
- Headings: Playfair Display
- Body: Source Serif 4
- UI elements: Inter

## Language
- English primary, with Swahili phrases used naturally (e.g. "Karibu Sana")
- Tone is pastoral, not corporate
